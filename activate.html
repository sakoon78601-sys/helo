❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my license key?

Your license key was sent to your email when you completed your purchase. Check your confirmation email or look in your profile dashboard.

How long is my license valid for?

License validity depends on your plan: Starter (1 month), Professional (3 months), or Premium (1 year). You can renew anytime before expiry.

Can I use one license on multiple devices?

Each license is for one business location. For multiple locations, you'll need separate licenses. Contact support for volume discounts.

What happens when my license expires?

You'll receive renewal reminders. You can renew your license anytime. There's a grace period of 7 days after expiry before access is blocked.

How do I renew my license?

Visit the pricing page and select your plan. You'll receive a new license key that extends your current license.
