❓ Partner Program FAQ

When do I get paid?

Commissions are calculated when the customer's payment is verified. You can request withdrawal anytime with minimum PKR 500 balance.

How do I get my referral link?

Register as a partner and you'll immediately get your unique referral link. Share it with your network!

Can I use multiple platforms?

Yes! Use your referral link on Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, blogs, or any platform. The same link tracks all conversions.

What if a customer cancels?

If a license is cancelled within 7 days of purchase, the commission is reversed. Otherwise, it's yours to keep.

How often can I withdraw?

You can request withdrawal anytime. Payouts are processed within 3-5 business days to your bank account.
